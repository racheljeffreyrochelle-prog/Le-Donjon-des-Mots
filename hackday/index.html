Le Donjon des Mots

I built a small Undertale-style phone game that helps a 10-year-old practise French between weekly lessons: numbers to 100, where I live, and the weather.

* HOW IT PLAYS

  • Every question is read aloud in French, with English in brackets.
  • Right answers fill a ♥. Full hearts unlock ⭐ ÉPARGNER to spare the monster.
  • Wrong answers bring a silly attack and a better hint. You can't lose.
  • "Répète !" invites the child to say the answer out loud.
  • Progress saves on the phone between sittings.

* DESIGNED FOR ONE KID

  • 5–10 minute sittings, at home or in the car.
  • Works with sound off, with a 🔊 replay on every question.
  • No time pressure, large tap targets, reduced motion respected.
  • No accounts, ads, tracking or microphone.
  • All characters and art are original.
