THE STORY

You're playing in the garden when you fall down a hole and land in le Donjon des Mots. The monsters down here have forgotten their words, and they're sad about it.

You don't fight them. You help them remember, one French answer at a time. When a monster is happy again, you spare it, and it lets you pass. Get past the great stone door and you're home.

FOUR ROOMS, FOUR FRIENDS

  • 👻BRUMETTE
    La Grotte · the cave

    A shy ghost who has forgotten her home. Tell her where you live: en ville, à la campagne, près de la mer…

  • 🐶CROQUETTE
    Le Pont · the bridge

    A dog with more bones than she can count. Help her count to 69: vingt et un, trente-deux…

  • 🐸RAINETTE
    La Mare · the pond

    A frog who dreams of seeing the world. Talk about the weather and places, and read her numbers.

  • 🗿MONSIEUR CENT
    La Grande Porte · the great door

    A stone giant who only lets you out if you can count to 100: soixante-dix, quatre-vingts, cent !

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Tap ▶ JOUER. Every line is read aloud in French, with the English underneath (like this).

  2. Answer the monster's question by tapping one of the choices. Each right answer fills a ♥.

  3. When all the hearts are full, tap ⭐ ÉPARGNER to spare the monster. It gives you a pie 🥧.

  4. When a monster says « Répète ! », say the French out loud. Nobody's checking, so be brave!

Wrong answers are fine. The monster does something silly, like licking your face, and you get a bigger hint. You can never lose.

FOR PARENTS

  • WHAT IT PRACTISESNumbers 1–100, with extra practice on the tricky ones: 70–99. Also where I live, places and the weather.
  • WHO IT'S FORChildren about 10 in their first years of school French, who know 1–20 and want to practise between lessons.
  • HOW LONGThe whole game takes one or two 5–10 minute sessions. Progress is saved on the phone between them.
  • PRIVACYNo accounts, no ads, no tracking and no microphone. Nothing about your child leaves the phone.
  • SOUNDBest with sound on: the phone's French voice reads everything aloud. It still works with sound off.
  • NO HELP NEEDEDYou don't need to speak French. English help is on screen all the way through.
